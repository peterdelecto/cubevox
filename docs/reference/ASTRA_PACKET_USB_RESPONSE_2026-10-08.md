1. **USB FS CDC and ROM DFU are feasible, but the packet is not sufficient for sign-off.** The stated PA11/PA12 routing, separate CC resistors and connector-side joins present no obvious enumeration blocker. The omitted USB supply, firmware initialization and silicon/ROM revisions still matter.

2. **Check VDD33USB explicitly.** It needs a decoupled 3.0–3.6 V supply; leaving it unpowered prevents USB operation. For this design, supply it from the board’s 3.3 V rail. ROM DFU does not support relying on `USBREGEN`. [Datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stm32h743zi.pdf), [AN2606 §60](https://www.st.com/resource/en/application_note/an2606-stm32microcontroller-system-memory-boot-mode-stmicroelectronics.pdf)

3. **The planned clock arithmetic is correct for revision V:** `25/5 × 192 = 960 MHz`, then `/2 = 480 MHz`, `/20 = 48 MHz`. Configure PLL1’s 4–8 MHz input range, wide VCO, Q output enable, USB mux, VOS0, flash latency and bus dividers. Older revision Y does not support this 480 MHz operating point. Set `HSE_VALUE=25000000` consistently. PLL3 then produces approximately **49.152008 MHz** with your parameters; its input-range setting must match 1 MHz. Uncorrected HSI48 is not a robust USB clock across operating conditions. [Datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stm32h743zi.pdf), [RM0433](https://www.st.com/resource/en/reference_manual/rm0433-stm32h742-stm32h743-753-and-stm32h750-value-line-advanced-armbased-32bit-mcus-stmicroelectronics.pdf)

4. **The application clock fix does not fix or configure ROM DFU.** H743 ROM versions from V13.3 onward use HSI48 with CRS for USB; your 25 MHz crystal is therefore not a DFU obstacle or requirement for those versions. V13.2 used HSE. Record the actual ROM version. [AN2606, Table 136](https://www.st.com/resource/en/application_note/an2606-stm32microcontroller-system-memory-boot-mode-stmicroelectronics.pdf)

5. **ROM caveats affect recovery and updating.** V9.0 can require an extra reset after power-up and can lock power configuration. V9.1 has a bank-2 erase-completion bug; V9.2 fixes it. PB15 held low and application-stack-pointer restrictions remain documented limitations. Test your exact `dfu-util` erase/write/leave sequence on the purchased silicon, including bank 2; retain physical reset recovery. Do not blindly add the documented PA11 pull-up workaround to a USB data line. [AN2606, Table 136](https://www.st.com/resource/en/application_note/an2606-stm32microcontroller-system-memory-boot-mode-stmicroelectronics.pdf)

6. **The backup-register magic needs your own early-startup handler.** ROM does not interpret `0xB00710AD`. Check and clear it before normal clock/USB initialization. `0x1FF09800` is the vector-table base: load MSP from `[base]`, branch through `[base+4]`, and establish the appropriate VTOR and interrupt state—not a direct function call to the base. Ensure USB interrupts are not left globally masked. Verify BOOT0 recovery option bytes separately. [ST boot-entry guidance](https://community.st.com/stm32-mcus-embedded-software-32/stm32h743-how-to-start-the-system-boot-loader-via-software-68068)

7. **Yes: gate attachment on VBUS.** Hold `DCTL.SDIS` asserted until PA9 reports VBUS; disconnect promptly when it disappears, and prevent automatic CDC startup from attaching first. Otherwise the device can signal into an unpowered host port and violate USB attachment requirements. Set self-powered descriptors/status correctly. Your application’s PA9 handling does not persist into ROM, which also uses PA9 for USART probing. [AN4879](https://www.st.com/content/st_com/en/technical-documents/an4879-introduction-to-usb-hardware-and-pcb-guidelines-using-stm32-mcus-stmicroelectronics_asset/chunk-003-topic-an4879-vbus-sensing-detection.html), [AN2606](https://www.st.com/resource/en/application_note/an2606-stm32microcontroller-system-memory-boot-mode-stmicroelectronics.pdf)

8. **Recheck the divider with barrel power absent.** At 5.25 V, 33k/82k produces approximately 3.74 V. Although AN4879 recommends these values, the H743 datasheet’s operating-input limit becomes 3.6 V when the relevant supplies are zero. Being below the 4 V absolute maximum is insufficient. Recalculate against VBUS/resistor tolerances and GPIO thresholds, or use an isolated MOSFET detector on a spare GPIO. Disable internal pulls. [Datasheet, Tables 112 and notes](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stm32h743zi.pdf)

9. **The ESD channel assignment is correct.** Use actual flow-through routing, an extremely short pin-2 ground return and local VBUS bypassing at pin 5; add 100 nF if C18 is not immediately adjacent. Check connector footprint numbering against its manufacturer drawing. Consider separate CC protection: D2 does not protect CC1/CC2. [USBLC6-2 datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/usblc6-2.pdf)

10. **Trace dimensions alone do not establish impedance.** Calculate approximately 90 Ω differential using the ordered JLC stackup. Seventy millimetres is not inherently excessive for FS. No external D+ pull-up or mandatory series resistors are needed; optional inline 0 Ω footprints near the MCU permit tuning. Avoid long test-point branches. [AN4879](https://www.st.com/resource/en/application_note/DM00296349-.pdf)

11. **The crystal capacitors remain unverified.** With equal 15 pF capacitors, effective load is `7.5 pF + Cstray`; matching 12 pF assumes 4.5 pF effective stray capacitance. Check crystal ESR, startup margin and drive level; retain R1 for adjustment. Provide an MCO measurement point instead of loading the crystal directly. [AN2867](https://www.st.com/resource/en/application_note/cd00221665-oscillator-design-guide-for-stm8afals-stm32-mcus-and-mpus-stmicroelectronics.pdf)

12. **Audit the shipped USB driver and test boundary cases.** ES0392 lists transmit-FIFO corruption and zero-length-packet errata relevant to device transfers; a clock fix does not address them. Check the bundled HAL’s workarounds, especially CDC transfers ending on 64-byte boundaries. Add VBUS, PA9, 3.3 V and ground test points. Validate both cable orientations, hubs, host sleep/wake, both power-up orders, CDC→DFU transitions and BOOT0 recovery. [ES0392 §2.25](https://www.st.com/resource/en/errata_sheet/es0392-stm32h742xig-stm32h743xig-stm32h750xb-stm32h753xi-device-errata-stmicroelectronics.pdf)
